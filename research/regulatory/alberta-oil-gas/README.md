# Alberta AER directional-survey templates

**REFERENCE / OPTIONAL EXPORT PROFILE — NOT ASSUMED TO APPLY TO MINING PROJECTS.**

These files are Alberta Energy Regulator (AER) OneStop directional-survey submission templates and validation rules. They are a useful local data-format and validation reference. They must not be treated as the regulatory regime for Brett's work or for DelvePath mining/directional-core-drilling users unless independently confirmed.

Source index: https://www.aer.ca/applications-and-notices/onestop/onestop-help
